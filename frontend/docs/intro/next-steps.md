## Additional Resources

As you continue your journey in Physical AI and humanoid robotics, consider exploring these resources:

### Community
- Join our Discord community for real-time discussions and support
- Participate in our GitHub discussions for technical questions
- Share your projects and get feedback from fellow learners

### Practice Projects
- Try implementing the examples in simulation before moving to hardware
- Join community challenges to test your skills
- Contribute to open-source robotics projects

### Advanced Topics
Once you've completed the 13-week journey, consider exploring:
- Advanced machine learning for robotics
- Computer vision and perception systems
- Reinforcement learning for robotic control
- Human-robot interaction design
