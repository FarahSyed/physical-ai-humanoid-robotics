## Course Structure Overview

The 13-week curriculum is designed to take you from foundational concepts to advanced humanoid development:

- **Weeks 1-2**: Introduction to Physical AI concepts and robotics fundamentals
- **Weeks 3-5**: ROS 2 (Robot Operating System) fundamentals and advanced concepts
- **Weeks 6-7**: Gazebo simulation environment and techniques
- **Weeks 8-10**: NVIDIA Isaac platform for robotics development
- **Weeks 11-12**: Humanoid robot development and control
- **Week 13**: Conversational AI and natural language interfaces for robots

Each week builds upon the previous, creating a comprehensive learning experience that progresses from simulation to real-world applications.
