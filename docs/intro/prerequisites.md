# Prerequisites

## Foundational Knowledge

### Core Concepts
- Understanding of basic physics and mechanics
- Familiarity with mathematical concepts (algebra, basic calculus)
- Basic understanding of sensors and actuators
- Knowledge of computer programming fundamentals

### Physical AI Concepts
- Basic understanding of artificial intelligence and machine learning
- Familiarity with perception and action systems
- Understanding of embodied cognition principles
- Knowledge of human-robot interaction concepts

## Recommended Background

### For Different Learning Paths

#### Beginners
- Start with fundamental robotics concepts before advancing to complex systems
- Focus on understanding the relationship between AI and physical systems
- Build intuition about how robots interact with the physical world

#### Intermediate Learners
- Leverage existing knowledge of robotics or AI to accelerate learning
- Focus on the integration of AI with physical systems
- Explore advanced control and perception systems

#### Advanced Practitioners
- Dive deeper into specialized topics in Physical AI
- Focus on complex humanoid control and interaction
- Explore cutting-edge research and applications

## Essential Skills

### Technical Skills
- Ability to understand and implement control systems
- Understanding of sensor fusion and perception
- Basic knowledge of simulation environments
- Familiarity with robot kinematics and dynamics

### Practical Skills
- Ability to troubleshoot and debug robotic systems
- Understanding of safety protocols for physical systems
- Knowledge of experimental design and validation
- Skills in analyzing robot behavior and performance

## Learning Path Recommendations

Based on your current knowledge level, we recommend:

- **Novice**: Start with Weeks 1-2 to build fundamental concepts
- **Intermediate**: Begin with Weeks 3-5 to build on existing knowledge
- **Advanced**: Jump to Weeks 8-10 for more complex implementations

## Physical AI Mindset

The most important prerequisite is developing a "Physical AI mindset":
- Thinking about AI in terms of physical interaction and embodiment
- Understanding the constraints and opportunities of real-world systems
- Appreciating the difference between digital and physical intelligence
- Embracing the challenges and rewards of embodied AI systems

This course is designed to be accessible to motivated learners at all levels. The key requirement is curiosity about the intersection of artificial intelligence and the physical world, and a willingness to engage with hands-on learning experiences.
